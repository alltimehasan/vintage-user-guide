Finding a bag

Copy link

We sell clothing by the bag: bags of vintage (secondhand) clothing and bags of new clothing, packed by hand in our Glasgow warehouse.

Choose Shop online in the menu to see every bag. On a phone, the menu is behind the Menu button.

The shop

  • Use All bags, Vintage and New to choose what you see.
  • Type in Search the bags to find a bag by name or what is in it, such as denim or jackets. Results appear as you type.
  • Each card shows the weight, roughly how many pieces, the sizes and the price. All prices include VAT.

A bag's page

Click a bag to see its photos and full description. Click a small photo to swap it in, or the main photo to see all the photos full screen: swipe, use the arrow buttons or your keyboard's arrow keys to move between them, and press the cross (or Esc) to close.

Vintage bags are secondhand, so the clothes show the signs of wear that come with their age. When a bag has sold out, it says Sold out. Press See bags in stock to look at others, or Ask us when it is back to send us a message.

Your basket

Copy link

Add as many bags as you like. Delivery is charged once per order, however many bags you buy.

When you press Add to basket you stay on the page: a message appears in the corner, the number beside Basket at the top goes up, and the bag shows how many are in your basket. In the shop, its button becomes In basket.

Press Basket at the top of any page to see your basket.

  • Use − and +, or type a number, to change how many. Press Remove to take a bag out.
  • The total includes VAT and delivery. Delivery is to UK addresses only.
  • If a bag sells out or runs low while it is in your basket, the basket tells you and adjusts itself.
  • Your basket is kept while you browse. It empties if you log out or leave the website for a long time.

Paying for your order

Copy link

Payment is taken by Stripe, a secure payment company. We never see or store your card number.

  1. In your basket, check the bags and the total, then press Pay now.
  2. Stripe's secure payment page opens. Enter your email, delivery address, phone number and card details. Depending on your device, Stripe may also offer Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  3. Press the pay button on Stripe's page.
  4. You come back to our website and see “Thank you for your order” with your order number. Your confirmation and invoice arrive by email.

Your bags are held for 35 minutes. While you are on the payment page, nobody else can buy your bags. If you take longer, the page expires: just go back to your basket and press Pay now again.

Paying faster next time

If you are logged in to your account, your basket shows who you are paying as and where it is going, and Stripe's page comes with your details filled in and your saved cards ready. There you can tick the box to save the card you pay with.

Not logged in? You can still pay as a guest; your account is made for you after your first order (see Your account).

If you change your mind on the payment page

Use the back link at the top of Stripe's page. You see “Payment not completed”: nothing was charged and your basket is just as you left it.

Payments that take a little longer

A few payment types take time to clear. Then the thank-you page says “We are confirming your payment with Stripe” and your confirmation follows by email once it has cleared. Your bags stay held for you meanwhile.

After you order

Copy link

You get your confirmation and invoice by email straight away, and you can follow your order in your account.

  • “Order confirmation and invoice …”: what you bought, the delivery address, your invoice as a PDF attachment, and how to cancel if you change your mind.
  • “Your Vintage Warehouse Glasgow account: set a password to see your orders”: on your first order only. Press Set your password within 24 hours to start using your account.

We pack your order and send it by courier. Your account shows Being packed while we prepare it and Dispatched once it has gone, with the date. If you need anything, contact us and quote your order number.

No email? Check your spam or junk folder first. Your invoice is also in your account, and you can always ask us to send the confirmation again.

Your account

Copy link

Your account keeps all your orders and invoices in one place, and saves your details and cards so paying takes seconds.

How you get one

  • Automatically with your first order. We make the account under the email you paid with and email you a link to set a password. All your orders with that email appear in it.
  • Or create one yourself. Choose Account → Create an account at the top of the website.

Creating an account

  1. Choose Account → Create an account.
  2. Enter your name and email, choose a password, and press Create account.
  3. We email you a link. Open the email “Verify your email address” and press the button within 60 minutes to confirm it is really your email.
  4. Your account is ready: you arrive on Your orders (or back in your basket if you came from there).

Choosing a password. Use at least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol. Passwords that have appeared in known data leaks are refused, so choose one you do not use anywhere else.

Ordered with us before? You already have an account, so “Create an account” will tell you so. Use Forgot your password? on the login page to choose a password.

Until you confirm your email, your account pages stay closed and your basket reminds you, with a link to get a new email if you need one.

Logging in

  1. Choose Account → Log in.
  2. Enter your email and password and press Log in. Tick Remember me on your own device to stay logged in.

Once you are logged in, the Account menu shows your orders, details, saved cards, password and Log out.

Forgotten your password, or never set one?

  1. On the login page, press Forgot your password?.
  2. Enter your email and press Email password reset link.
  3. Open the email “Reset your password”, press Reset Password and choose a new password. The link lasts 24 hours.
  4. Log in with your new password.

Your details

Copy link

Account → Your details. We fill these in on the payment page for you, so you only need to check them and pay.

  • Name: the name on your orders and the parcel.
  • Phone (optional): in case the courier needs to reach you.
  • Delivery address: UK only. Postcodes are tidied for you (“g140bx” becomes “G14 0BX”). Leave every line blank to remove the saved address.
  • Press Save details.

Changing your email address

  1. Under Email address, enter the new address and your password, and press Send the link.
  2. Open the email we send to the new address and press Confirm this email address within an hour, while logged in to your account.
  3. Your account now uses the new address for logging in and order emails. We let your old address know too.

Nothing changes until you open the link, so a typing mistake cannot lock you out.

8

Saved cards

Copy link

Account → Saved cards. A saved card is ready and waiting on the payment page, so paying takes one step.

  • To save a card while paying: log in first, then tick the box on Stripe's payment page to save your card.
  • To add one now: press Add a card. Stripe's secure page opens; enter the card and you come back with it saved.
  • To remove one: press Remove beside it. It is removed straight away.

Your card is safe. Cards are kept by Stripe, our payment provider. We never see or store a card number: only the type of card, its last four digits and when it expires. Cards can only be saved by a logged-in customer with a confirmed email, never when paying as a guest.

Password and logging out

Copy link

Account → Password to change your password; Account → Log out when you are done.

  • Enter your current password, then the new one twice, and press Change password. Any other device logged in to your account is logged out.
  • On a shared computer, always log out. Logging out also empties the basket and closes any payment page you had open, so nobody else can use your saved cards.

Delivery

Copy link
  • We deliver to UK addresses only, by courier.
  • Delivery is one charge per order, however many bags you buy. It is shown next to every price, in your basket and on the payment page before you pay.
  • How long it takes is shown under the Add to basket button and in your basket, for example “Dispatched within 2 working days. Delivered by courier in 2–3 working days.”
  • Our Delivery and returns page (at the bottom of every page of the website) has the details.
11

Cancelling and returns

Copy link

You have 14 days after delivery to cancel your order for any reason.

  1. Tell us within 14 days of receiving your order, quoting your order or invoice number. Email us, use the contact form, or fill in the Model cancellation form on our website.
  2. Send the bag back as our Delivery and returns page explains.
  3. We refund you to the card you paid with. Your account then shows the order as refunded.

If something is faulty or not as described, contact us and we will put it right. Please remember that vintage clothing is secondhand and shows the wear that comes with its age; that is not a fault. Your order confirmation email also explains your right to cancel and your statutory rights.

Contact us and visiting

Copy link

Send us a message any time, or come and pick and choose in the warehouse.

  1. Choose Contact us in the menu.
  2. Enter your name, email, phone (optional) and your message (at least 10 characters).
  3. Press Send message. You see “Message sent”, and we reply to your email as soon as we can.

Visiting the warehouse

We are in Bay E of the Barclay Curle Complex, South Street, Glasgow G14 0BX. Find us in the menu shows our opening hours and a Get directions button. The map loads only when you press Load map, because it comes from Google.

Prefer a dark screen? Press the moon icon at the top of any page (or Dark mode in the phone menu). The website remembers your choice on that device.

Questions

Copy link
Do I need an account to order?

No. You can pay as a guest. We make your account with your first order, so you can follow it; just set a password from the email we send.

Is it safe to pay on your website?

Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, a secure payment company used by millions of businesses. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see your card number.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

No, online orders are delivered to UK addresses only. You are welcome to visit the warehouse in person.

When will my order arrive?

The delivery times are shown under the Add to basket button and in your basket. Your account shows when we have dispatched it.

I didn't get my confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder. Your order and invoice are also in your account under Your orders. If you still need the email, contact us and we will send it again.

A bag disappeared from my basket

Our bags are often one of a kind. If one sells out or runs low while it is in your basket, the basket removes or reduces it and tells you what changed.

The payment page expired, or says payment not completed

Nothing was charged. Go back to your basket and press Pay now again. The payment page holds your bags for 35 minutes.

I used the wrong delivery address

Contact us as soon as possible, quoting your order number, and we will sort it out before we send it.

Do I get a VAT invoice?

Yes. It is attached to your confirmation email, and you can open it any time from your account with View invoice.

How do I delete my account?

Reply to the account email we sent you, or contact us, and we will delete it. Your past orders stay on our records, as the law requires.
