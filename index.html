Before the online shop opens

The website takes payments only once the first four are done. The dashboard lists anything still missing.

  • Set the UK delivery charge (or £0.00 for free delivery). Settings → Shop
  • Enter the notification email that new orders and messages go to. Settings → Shop
  • Enter the shop's public email address. The law requires one on the website. Settings → Business
  • Ask the developer to add the Stripe keys, so the website can take card payments. What the developer does
  • Replace the six placeholder bags with real ones: names, photos, weights and prices. Adding a bag
  • Read and approve the legal pages (privacy, terms, delivery and returns, cancellation form), then delete the draft line at the top of each. Legal pages
  • Check the opening hours, phone number, Instagram and TikTok links. Settings → Business
  • Turn on two-factor login for every staff account, and invite your colleagues. Your own account
